Established for over a decade, Company Services is a leading staffing and recruitment agency. With a keen focus on the unique needs of our clients, we are dedicated to providing a full range of turnkey recruitment solutions that serve your every business need.

As a staffing solutions provider, Company offers a full spectrum of recruitment and related services that include:
Staff Placement

Our staff placement services include executive search, permanent/temporary as well as contract placements.

Payroll Services

For companies seeking all-in-one convenience, we offer payroll services that allow clients flexibility in their task allocation and enhanced work productivity.

Work Pass Application

We are also able to act on behalf of clients in applying for Employment Passes, S-Passes and Work Permits.

We offer better fits by taking time to truly understand the needs of both clients and candidates, striving to foster enduring relationships that we believe will lead to the best solutions.

At Company, the interest of both clients and candidates are paramount, which explains why we have built a team that takes immense pride in service. Comprising of committed and proficient consultants with varied industry experiences, we offer personalised services that go beyond the mundane.
